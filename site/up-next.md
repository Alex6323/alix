- **0.9 is the sync release.** Put any workspace or deck from your desktop on
  your paired phone, review it offline with the progress you already earned,
  and hand the progress back. The first cut is on main.
- **Sync from anywhere** over your own Tailscale mesh: alix prints a second
  pairing address for the mesh, and the phone syncs wherever it is while
  the desktop runs alix. No engine, no accounts, nothing leaves your
  devices.
- **Prompt hygiene under the hood.** A hard boundary between alix's own
  prompt text and deck text in every AI call, the exam's source fence, and
  context that keeps quotes, tables, and checklists as structure instead of
  flattening them to text.
