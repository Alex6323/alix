# Verbs of arguing

Every card in this section shares this explanation. It is intentionally long
enough to demonstrate why optional orientation must not displace the question
or crowd the answer choices during an ordinary review.

## Which German verb means "to advocate"?
<!-- id: card-2kdq2rmxgjz93sdpd3xne603w0 -->
<!-- choices-single -->
- [x] befürworten
- [ ] einräumen
- [ ] unterstellen
- [ ] widerlegen
> Einräumen means to concede, unterstellen means to allege, and widerlegen means to refute.
